↓ 02 · The Problem

tell you it's right.

Ask a single LLM a hard question and you get one voice: polished, self-assured, and impossible to audit. No sources you can open, no sense of what it got wrong.

↓ 03 · The Solution

Research beyond answers.

POLYNOUS is a 7-agent AI research & debate platform that turns any question into a cited, confidence-scored report (or a rubric-judged debate) instead of one unverifiable answer.

Neural Research
A cited report, not a guess
Streamed live with inline citations, per-claim confidence, contradiction flags, and honest cost telemetry.
Debate Chamber
A rubric-scored verdict
FOR vs AGAINST argue it out; an impartial Judge scores each side 1–10 with transparent reasoning.
↓ 04 · How It Works

A team, not a guess.

A live 5-stage pipeline, streamed token-by-token. Each stage checks the last.

CRITIC
Cross-references sources & flags contradictions.
DEEPEN
Auto-runs follow-up searches on coverage gaps.
WRITER
Synthesizes a cited, confidence-scored report.
↓ 05 · The Differentiator

Two sides.
One rubric-scored verdict.

For contested questions, FOR and AGAINST build evidence-backed cases; an impartial Judge delivers a 1–10 verdict you can re-weight, re-frame, cross-examine, or join yourself.

trending_up Where the model goes at scale
Managed credits
Once we have the user base, we buy model credits in bulk and hand each user a set number of questions per plan. BYOK stops being required.
Metered tiers
Simple quotas (e.g. 50 / 300 / unlimited questions per month) at fixed rates. No API keys, no setup, one bill.
BYOK + platform fee
Power users can still bring their own key, but pay a small platform fee on top for the orchestration, agents & graph.
